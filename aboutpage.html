About - Home, But Online.

This website was made just for two people, to unite them even with the distance between them. It follows their story through some very exhausting days to some very exciting days. The main motive was to make a place where they both can spend their time rather than being stuck with the same boring days. This place will never be finished, as their journey has just begun. Soon, it’s going to be home to hundreds and thousands of stories, plans, and memories. It will be constantly monitored and updated throughout; old things will get better, and new things will be added to make their experience as good as it can be. A lot of effort was given to make this place possible. Thousands of lines of code, hundreds of art styles, and tens of videos have contributed to this little place on the internet that they call home.
