Poem Title

Nothing to see here yet.

Added on: 30 June 2026
Close

Okay, so I don't know where to start, to be honest. I've never written a big letter before, especially not for a website. I'll try my best, so here we go. Firstly, I just want to thank you for being in my life. You've become a part of my life—and honestly, that's an understatement. You've become a major part of it. The day we first met, I had no idea what was going to happen. Life is so silly sometimes, right? It teases you, punishes you, and sometimes rewards you with something that's going to change everything in your life. For me, that came in the form of a girl like you. The moment we started talking is something I would love to relive once again. All the time we spent together talking, playing games, making fun of each other, watching movies, and trying our best to love each other from so far away... those are some of my favorite memories. Long distance isn't easy, right? You know it, I know it, and everyone knows it too. People usually say, "Ugh, long distance sucks. It always ends in a week or a month," or something like that. But I'm not here to prove them wrong or right. I'm here because I know I have a purpose—a purpose to spend my life with you. And I don't like glazing, but this time I'm keeping that side of me aside, so get outta here, overthinker Aadarsh. I just want to talk about you. You are so sweet, loving, caring, and kind. I don't know if I say this enough, but you make me proud every single day. You deserve every bit of happiness and every bit of love in this world, and I hope that I'm always a part of that list too. I don't want to make this too emotional, but who am I kidding? I don't even think this is enough to make someone cry. I don't know if you're recording yourself while reading this, but if you are, please do smile for me. Even though I can't see it right now, I can definitely feel it. That red string theory you told me about... I think there's a little truth to it. I might run out of words, but I'll never run out of feelings for you. After months of work, I can finally give you something—a place made just for us. A place for our memories, our moments, and everything in between. I really hope you like it. Please do let me know what you think, and enjoy your stay. Explore a little, take your time, and I'll see you soon. I love you so muchh.
